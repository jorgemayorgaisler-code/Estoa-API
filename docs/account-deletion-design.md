# Account deletion — proposed design, not enabled
Require recent reauthentication and explicit confirmation. Record a deletion request with an opaque identifier and timestamp. Revoke sessions and disable access atomically once confirmed. Block new checkout and trial activation for disabled accounts. Reconcile any pending checkout and active subscription with the payment provider; disabling an account alone does not cancel recurring charges. Never report cancellation until authoritative confirmation. Retain only legally necessary accounting and contractual evidence under a reviewed retention policy; pseudonymize identifiers where permitted. Remove unnecessary profile data and credentials after reconciliation. Prevent restoration from backups from silently reactivating deleted accounts. Do not implement irreversible deletion or retention periods until Chilean legal requirements and provider cancellation behavior are verified. Test concurrent login, checkout, payment notification, duplicate requests, failed cancellation, and backup restoration. No production endpoint or automated deletion job is enabled.
